Struggling with self-esteem:
Comparing oneself to peers around appearance, achievements, or abilities is extremely common in college, especially with constant exposure to others on social media. Self-worth based on a single comparison point tends to feel especially harsh; it can help to notice when comparison is happening and gently redirect attention back to one's own effort or progress instead.

Body image concerns:
Feeling insecure about one's body, height, skin color, or appearance is a common experience and is often shaped by unrealistic standards seen online or around oneself, not a personal shortcoming. These feelings are valid, and talking about them with someone trusted, rather than carrying them alone, often makes them feel lighter.

Feeling not good enough:
The feeling of not measuring up to others, academically or personally, is common and does not reflect a person's actual worth or potential. It can help to notice this thought pattern when it shows up and ask whether the same harsh judgment would be applied to a friend in the same situation.
