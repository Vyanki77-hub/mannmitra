How to reach the Division of Happiness and Wellbeing:
Students can access LPU's Department of Counseling and Happiness by booking an appointment in advance through the University Management System (UMS) or by directly visiting the department for a walk-in counselling session. The department is located at Building 8, Room 208. The current timings listed by LPU are Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. LPU does not publicly specify an expected waiting time for counselling sessions.

What counselling support is confidential:
LPU's Department of Counseling and Happiness provides free and confidential professional counselling and psychological support to students. Individual and group counselling sessions are conducted in a confidential manner, providing students with a safe space to discuss concerns such as stress, anxiety, interpersonal conflicts, self-esteem issues, academic difficulties and other personal or emotional challenges. LPU also states that students can seek counselling voluntarily, while referrals may also come from faculty members, wardens, friends or parents.

What to do in a mental health emergency on campus:
For an immediate medical or mental-health emergency on campus, students should seek urgent assistance from LPU's 24/7 Uni-Health Centre and medical emergency services. LPU states that the Uni-Health Centre provides 24/7 OPD and emergency services and has ambulances available 24/7. The official health and well-being framework lists the Medical Emergency contact numbers as 9878426789 and 987842680. For student well-being and crisis-response concerns, LPU also lists Dr. Vishal Sharma as the Nodal Officer for Student Well-Being, Crisis Response and Compliance, with email dsr@lpu.co.in and mobile number +91-1824-520150. LPU has also partnered with YourDOST to provide students with confidential online counselling support available 24/7, 365 days a year.

Support available for academic stress:
LPU provides academic support through its mentoring system, Additional Tutorial Classes, Remedial Sessions and Self-Learning Materials. Every LPU student is paired with a mentor who provides academic and personal guidance. Students struggling with difficult courses or preparing for examinations can receive additional academic support through tutorial and remedial sessions, while Self-Learning Materials provide resources for independent study. LPU's Department of Counseling and Happiness also provides support for students dealing with academic stress, including individual counselling, group counselling, workshops and stress-management programmes.
