How to request an extension or academic accommodation:
LPU's general public website does not provide a sufficiently detailed, universally applicable procedure for requesting an academic extension or accommodation due to personal or medical circumstances. Students should check their UMS announcements and contact their School/Division or the relevant academic authority for the procedure applicable to their programme and situation. The required documents, approval authority and permitted extension may depend on the specific academic or examination matter.

Where to get study support or tutoring:
LPU provides academic support to students facing academic difficulties through Remedial/Recoup Classes, Self Learning Materials (SLM), and additional tutoring support. Remedial/Recoup Classes are intended to help students revise difficult topics, bridge gaps in learning and prepare for Reappear Examinations. Self Learning Materials provide self-directed learning resources, including explanations, self-check questions, exercises and worksheets for selected courses. LPU also provides additional tutoring classes to support students who have difficulty understanding their current-term courses.
